📖 How Does CalAge Work?

CalAge calculates your age by comparing your date of birth with the current date.

The calculator determines the completed number of years, months and days between the two dates.

The date difference calculator can also calculate the approximate number of days, weeks and hours between two selected dates.

Important: CalAge is intended for general informational and calculation purposes. For important official calculations, always verify the result independently.

⏳ Age in Days, Hours & Minutes

Your age can be represented using different units of time. CalAge provides an approximate total number of days, hours and minutes based on your date of birth.

These values are approximate because the calculator uses calendar dates rather than the exact time of birth.
